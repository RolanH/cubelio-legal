2. What CubeGym processes and where

CubeGym is an adaptive speedcubing learning app. Learning records and history are stored locally on the iPad; cloud copies are added when using a parent account and authorised synchronisation. Local profiles not adopted into an account remain local.

Leagues are hidden in the first version. Granting new league permission is not possible. Previously granted permissions from the internal beta remain stored and can be withdrawn at any time through “Withdraw league permission” in the parent area. This action appears only for currently granted permission. The child sees no league in this app version. The mosaic and fixed nickname remain the profile picture and profile name and are synchronised within the family.

Hiding leagues does not change server processing. The server continues to derive league-eligible XP from synchronised learning events, independently of league permission. Weekly XP is calculated from it. When weekly reassignment runs, weekly XP for existing memberships is stored, rank and promotion/demotion are determined for profiles still enabled, and non-deleted profiles with active league permission are assigned for the following week. Authorised participants with active permission can still receive nicknames, mosaics and weekly XP. For children, current child-cloud consent is additionally required. Whether and when weekly reassignment actually runs in operation remains to be checked.

4. Local storage and section 25 TDDDG

The iPad holds learning history and settings in a local database, the cube cache in local app storage, and authentication/PIN data and the deletion journal in the Keychain. Keychain values are configured as device-bound without iCloud synchronisation, but may survive reinstallation. The behaviour of other operating-system device backups remains to be checked.

For storage and access strictly necessary for explicitly requested offline learning, authentication, PIN protection or pairing, the exception in section 25(2)(2) TDDDG is proposed. Non-essential storage and access may require prior consent under section 25(1), in addition to a GDPR basis. The local diagnostic buffer starts automatically with the app; seen cube names are redacted in it. Only sharing is voluntary. Whether this automatic storage is strictly necessary or requires prior consent remains to be checked, as does future storage by integrated software. Voluntary sharing and operating-system permissions do not replace this assessment.

5. Bluetooth, camera, tracking and advertising

Bluetooth connects to the smart cube and receives its moves and states. No Bluetooth background mode is enabled; Bluetooth is marked as not used for location. The camera is used only to read a pairing QR code; camera images are not uploaded to the cloud. Microphone access is not requested. Manual code entry remains available if camera permission is refused.

CubeGym provides for no advertising and no advertising tracking. Advertising attribution and collection of Apple advertising or vendor identifiers IDFA/IDFV through the corresponding additional calls are not intended. Learning events and RevenueCat billing analytics are distinct from this. Actual behaviour of the integrated software in the finished app build remains to be checked. “No tracking” does not mean “no processing”.

6. Recipients, processors and international transfers

Supabase: Authentication, database, synchronisation and server functions. The intended EU database region is Frankfurt (eu-central-1), currently on the Free plan. EU hosting does not guarantee that all operational and support processing remains in the EU. Supabase describes additional processing and standard contractual clauses. The contracting entity, data processing agreement, subprocessors and transfer assessment remain to be checked. Further information: Supabase on data regions and transfers, data processing agreement and subprocessors.

RevenueCat, Inc., USA: When purchase integration is enabled, RevenueCat manages subscriptions and entitlements and performs billing analytics as a processor. Technical and transaction data is sent to the USA; its data processing agreement provides standard contractual clauses for relevant transfers. Participation in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework has not been verified; no transfer assurance is based on it. Contractual basis, transfer risks and retention require review before activation. The purchase integration is included. On account deletion, the server attempts RevenueCat customer deletion; provider cleanup remains open if configuration is missing or an error occurs. Further information: RevenueCat privacy policy and data processing agreement.

The parent account UUID is used as the custom user identifier for RevenueCat. According to the provider, it processes purchase data and technical information such as device type, operating system and last usage. The integrated software reportedly sends Apple’s tracking permission status (ATT) without prompting for permission. Additional calls for advertising attribution or device attributes are not intended. Automatic behaviour and IP server logs remain to be checked before ruling out further identifiers.

Apple: App Store, purchase handling and its own TestFlight/crash reports. Apple is not characterised as our processor across the board. TestFlight automatically collects crash and usage data and shares it with the developer; tester contact details and comments may also be included. Store crash reports depend on diagnostic sharing. CubeGym uses Apple’s built-in crash channel. The legal basis and retention for our own report downloads remain to be checked. Further information: Apple privacy policy and TestFlight & Privacy.

Other league participants: Despite the hidden league view, authorised participants with existing beta permissions can still receive fixed nicknames, mosaics and weekly XP. Current permissions and authorised membership are required, as described in section 2. Hiding the view does not generally block other families’ server access.

Operations and backups: Encrypted local database backups exist. Other technical operational services and their possible processing of learning data, as well as support hosting and associated logs, remain to be checked. Hosting of these webpages is described under “These pages”.

7. Children and the parent area

Children need no personal email in their learning profile. Nicknames come from a fixed list; avatars have nine mosaic cells with six colours plus empty. The learning and league areas have no free text, photos or chat. Parents control cloud consent and can separately withdraw existing league permissions from the internal beta. New league permissions are not possible in the first version; “Withdraw league permission” appears only for currently granted permission. The child sees no league in this app version. Purchases belong behind the protected parental access. The PIN protects the parent area but does not replace legally valid consent or age/parent verification. The method requires legal review before the public launch.

To be checked before US launch: Classification under the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), verifiable parental consent, direct parental notice, handling of third parties and research, and written retention and security rules. This draft, store category, PIN, trial or purchase does not establish COPPA compliance. Further information: FTC on COPPA.

8. Adaptive learning logic

CubeGym derives a learning state from attempts, answers, times and moves and selects suitable tasks. The learning system estimates learning needs and practice intervals; the results are not legal or school assessment decisions. Based on the described product scope, there is no solely automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects within Article 22 GDPR. This classification remains subject to legal review.

10. Your rights, export, deletion and withdrawal

Subject to the legal conditions, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and complaint to a supervisory authority. The controller and contact details will be added before the public launch. Consent may be withdrawn for the future; the lawfulness of earlier processing is unaffected.

In the parent area’s Data & Account (Daten & Konto) section, you can view stored data, request a JSON account export, and delete the account. Profiles can be deleted separately. The export contains personal account data and learning history; it is not a research export or a complete retrieval of all data held by Apple or RevenueCat. Further access may be requested from the controller once contact details have been added.

Child-cloud, research and league permissions may be withdrawn separately. Existing league permissions from the internal beta remain withdrawable at any time in the parent area; granting new permission is not possible in the first version. Hiding leagues alone changes neither stored permissions nor server processing. League withdrawal blocks league display through the server and prevents further weekly assignments, but does not automatically erase existing XP and membership data. Child-cloud withdrawal blocks new collection and device access; research exports are checked against current consent. Technically, withdrawal alone does not automatically delete existing personal learning events; profile or account deletion is additionally available.

After withdrawal, we review further storage and delete the data unless an independent legal basis or statutory exception applies. No additional erasure request is required. The necessary review and deletion procedure is still missing and must be legally clarified before the public launch. See EDPB Guidelines on consent, paragraphs 117–119.

A successful account deletion removes the associated account-bound live data and bound authentication users, with these exceptions: global refresh and webhook deduplication receipts remain without an automatic deletion period; refresh IDs contain the parent account UUID.

On this device, deleting a parent account also removes that account’s access-cache entry, pending purchase marker and trial anchor including the legacy account assignment. Failed local cleanup remains in the deletion journal and is retried on next start. Sign-out does not erase these stored access data; other accounts’ trial anchors and the install-wide device time remain. Deleting the app removes all these local access data.

The 90-day validity applies only to the separate account deletion receipts, not to global billing receipts or the local access cache (section 9). Profile deletion leaves a minimal marker and consent receipts until account deletion. Existing offline copies, provider residual copies and our monthly backups need separate cleanup. Immediate remote erasure on offline devices is not promised.

Subscriptions must be cancelled separately in the App Store. The server attempts RevenueCat customer deletion and reports its status; successful account deletion does not confirm complete provider cleanup.
